Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

SortMyTrade (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a home-improvement and trades coordination service operating across South Manchester & Cheshire. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and your rights over it.

Who we are

SortMyTrade, operated by Zaino Leles. You can contact us at any time via WhatsApp on 07459 819603 or through sortmytrade.com.

What we collect

When you enquire through our website or forms, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details (phone number, and email if provided)
  • Your postcode / property location
  • Details of the work you’re enquiring about (project type, budget range, timeline, photos you choose to share)

Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide a quote
  • To arrange and coordinate the work with our vetted trades
  • To keep you updated about your project
  • To improve our service and understand where our enquiries come from

How we use and share it

We share only the information necessary with the vetted tradesperson matched to your job, so they can carry out the work. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for unrelated marketing. Where a job is referred to a partner contractor, relevant details are shared with them to deliver the work.

How we store it

Your information is held securely and kept only as long as needed to provide our service and meet any legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect it from unauthorised access.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you have the right to access the information we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, and to object to its use. To exercise any of these, just contact us on the details above.

Cookies & tracking

Our website may use cookies and analytics (including Google and Meta advertising tools) to understand how visitors use the site and measure the performance of our advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
