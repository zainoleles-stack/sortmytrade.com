The SortMyTrade Promise

Every job comes with one fixed price agreed upfront — and if anything isn't right, we put it right at our cost, not yours. One point of contact from start to finish. That's the deal.

The problem we solve

Finding one good tradesperson is hard. Finding several — and getting them to turn up and finish properly — is a nightmare.

Most people don't have a "plumber problem." They have a house full of jobs and no time to find, vet and chase the right people for each one. That's what we take off your hands — you tell us the job, we coordinate the right trades, and give you one fixed price and one person to deal with. If something isn't right, we put it right.
