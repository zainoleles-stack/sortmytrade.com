Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

SortMyTrade (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a home-improvement and trades coordination service operating across South Manchester & Cheshire. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights over it.

Who we are

SortMyTrade is run by Zaino Leles, who is the data controller for the information described here. Contact us at any time via WhatsApp on 07459 819603 or through sortmytrade.com.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details you give us: your name, phone number, email (if provided), postcode, a description of the job, budget range, timeline, and any photos you send.
  • Job records: if you go ahead, the scope, quotes, invoices, payments and messages about your job.
  • Advert click information: if you reach our website from a Google advert, a click reference (“gclid”) is sent with your enquiry so we can tell which adverts lead to enquiries and jobs.
  • Cookies: only if you accept them (see Cookies below).

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To answer your enquiry, quote and arrange the work: because you asked us to, and to take steps towards a contract with you.
  • To carry out and manage your job, take payment and keep you updated: to perform our contract with you.
  • To keep accounts and records: to meet our legal obligations.
  • To follow up on an enquiry, ask for a review once a job is finished, and understand which of our adverts work: our legitimate interest in running and improving the business. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • Advertising cookies: only with your consent.

Who we share it with

  • The tradespeople and suppliers matched to your job, only what they need to quote for and carry out the work (for example your name, number, address and the job details).
  • Formspree, which delivers website form submissions to our inbox.
  • Google, for our advertising. When an enquiry from a Google advert becomes a paid job, we may tell Google Ads the click reference, the date and the job value so it can show our adverts to people more likely to need us. We don’t send your name or contact details for this.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), when you choose to message us there.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use legally recognised safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don’t become a job: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Customer and job records: 6 years after the job, for accounting and legal purposes.
  • The advert click reference saved in your browser (only if you accept cookies): 90 days.

Cookies

Our website uses Google Ads to measure which adverts lead to enquiries. Advertising cookies are off until you choose “Accept” on the banner. If you choose “Reject”, Google may still receive basic, cookieless signals (for example that an enquiry form was sent) without identifying you.

  • smt_consent (stored in your browser): remembers your choice so we don’t ask again.
  • smt_click (stored in your browser, only if you accept): the Google advert click reference, kept for 90 days.
  • Google Ads cookies such as _gcl_au (only if you accept): measure conversions from our adverts.

You can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” at the bottom of our pages, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you have the right to access the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to how we use it (including for marketing and advert measurement), and withdraw consent at any time. To use any of these rights, contact us on the details above. We’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113. We’d appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
